# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive and inclusive. Welcome people regardless of
experience, disability, age, race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender,
gender identity or sexual orientation.

Critique ideas and code, not people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
unwanted sexual attention and publishing private information are unacceptable.
Respect confidentiality and maintain professional conduct in issues, pull
requests and other project spaces.

The maintainer may edit/remove inappropriate material, limit participation or
ban repeated or severe offenders, applying these rules fairly. Maintainers are
subject to the same standards.

Report concerns privately through the repository owner's
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/jacek4yang) contact options. Do not post
sensitive personal details publicly. If no private channel is available, open
a brief issue requesting a confidential contact without disclosing the incident.
